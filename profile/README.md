Explore a new world of gig opportunities with 'Bizzzy'—your mobile platform for finding jobs and increasing your earnings. 

Check out [website](https://orchlon.dev) for download links and previews.


### Features: (Bizzzy application)

- **Create your Handy profile**: Create a professional profile to showcase handyman skills and attract new gig opportunities. 
- **Receive gig jobs from client**: A handyman may receive job requests from users who are impressed by their experience.
- **Handyman can submit service offers for review**: If a user submits a job request with a lower proposed payment, the handyman can submit a counteroffer. Both parties can negotiate until a mutual agreement is reached.
- **Cross-Platform Compatibility**: Access 'Bizzzy' on any device, anytime, anywhere.
- **Real-Time chat between user and handyman**: Communicate with users in real time, with the ability to exchange images, videos, and voice messages.
